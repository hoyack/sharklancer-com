The pilot path

From workflow drag to managed automation.

Thunderstaff is the product. Hoyack scopes, builds, and operates the work.

  1. Locate the bottleneck

    Bring one repetitive workflow, the people it touches, and the outcome that matters.

  2. Set a bounded pilot

    Agree on scope, access, human approval points, safeguards, and a measurable success signal.

  3. Build and observe

    Hoyack implements the automation and validates it against real operating conditions.

  4. Operate or expand

    Keep the workflow managed, improve it with evidence, and expand only where the case is clear.
