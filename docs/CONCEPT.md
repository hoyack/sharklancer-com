# One-page concept brief

## Audience and problem

Sharklancer serves SMB operators in service, trade, and light-industrial businesses who can identify costly repetitive work but do not want a long AI strategy exercise. Their problem is not a lack of AI ideas; it is translating one operational bottleneck into a bounded, supportable automation.

## Positioning

A sharp, inbound signal route into Thunderstaff managed AI automation. The hunters metaphor lives in the visual language—radar, signal, target—not in a staffing or roster product. Thunderstaff is the product and Hoyack fulfills the work.

## Information architecture

1. Hero: direct value and pilot CTA
2. Fit signals: recognizable workflow patterns
3. Pilot path: discover, bound, build, operate
4. Product boundary: explicit is/is-not framing
5. Call preparation: reduce booking friction
6. Final CTA and legal/ownership disclosure

## Visual direction

A dark operational console with acid signal green, coral alert marks, radar geometry, editorial serif headlines, and compact monospace labels. The design avoids generic SaaS gradients and marketplace cards.

## Content pillars

- Workflow signal recognition
- Bounded pilot design
- Human approval and operational safeguards
- Managed implementation and continuous improvement

## CTA and revenue hypothesis

Primary conversion: “Book a Thunderstaff pilot call.” Attribution: `source:sharklancer`, `offer:thunderstaff` via UTMs and click events. Revenue occurs only if a qualified call becomes a separately scoped Thunderstaff pilot or managed-services engagement delivered by Hoyack. No Sharklancer pricing layer.
