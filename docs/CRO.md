# CRO conversion and distribution contract

## ICP

Owner-operators, operations leaders, and functional managers at SMBs with recurring workflow volume, system handoffs, and authority to sponsor a bounded pilot.

## Offer and event

- Offer: Thunderstaff managed AI automation pilot
- Primary conversion: completed Calendly booking
- Micro-conversions: header, hero, and final CTA clicks; Thunderstaff referral click
- Attribution: `source:sharklancer`, `offer:thunderstaff`, campaign `thunderstaff-pilot`

## Revenue mechanism

Hoyack earns pilot and/or managed-service revenue under a signed agreement. There is no Sharklancer marketplace, placement fee, or separate pricing stack.

## 30/60/90 distribution

- 0–30 days: direct navigation, Hoyack portfolio cross-links, controlled founder/partner referrals; baseline CTA CTR and booked-call quality.
- 31–60 days: publish two workflow-signal notes and test a single hero proof module only after evidence is approved.
- 61–90 days: expand only the highest-quality inbound source; add a case note after client permission and substantiation.

No cold outreach under the Sharklancer identity. Inbound only.
