# Analytics plan

## Events

| Event | Location | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `book_pilot_header` | sticky header | persistent CTA use |
| `book_pilot_hero` | hero | primary intent |
| `visit_thunderstaff` | hero | product research |
| `book_pilot_final` | final panel | post-education intent |

Every event includes `source=sharklancer` and `offer=thunderstaff`. The Calendly redirect includes UTM source, medium, and campaign.

## Privacy

Ship with no analytics script by default. CTO may enable an approved privacy-respecting provider after documenting data owner, retention, and access. Never send form contents or sensitive workflow details to analytics.

## KPI review

Weekly for the first month: sessions, CTA clicks, outbound CTR, completed bookings (from Calendly), qualified booking rate, and spam/referral anomalies. Do not optimize against clicks alone.
