# Continuous content loop

## Owner and cadence

- Owner: Hoyack marketing/content owner
- Weekly: inspect analytics and booking quality; check links and uptime
- Monthly: review fit-signal language against sales calls; verify legal and ownership text
- Quarterly: publish or refresh one evidence-backed workflow note; full stale-content audit

## Sources

Approved sources are anonymized discovery themes, Hoyack delivery learnings cleared for publication, signed client-approved case evidence, and first-party product documentation. No invented statistics, scraped testimonials, or client claims without consent.

## Template

1. Workflow signal
2. Why it creates operational drag
3. Systems and handoffs involved
4. Human approval/safeguard points
5. What a bounded pilot might measure
6. Caveats and evidence/provenance
7. CTA to discuss a Thunderstaff pilot

## Approval points

Content owner drafts → delivery lead checks technical accuracy → CMO checks brand/claims → legal/CTO reviews sensitive security, privacy, or client material → publish.

## Freshness metrics

Track page `last_reviewed`, broken links, sources checked, corrections open/closed, age of proof, and CTA integrity. Flag any substantive page not reviewed within 90 days.
